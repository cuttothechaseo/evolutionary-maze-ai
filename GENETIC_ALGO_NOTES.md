Title: Genetic Algorithms in Python - Evolution For Optimization

# Representation / Population -> what you are genetically modifying
## 3 operations to the population
- Reproduction
- Mutation
- Selection

# Fitness Function
"How good is the solution" (could be sum of numbers in population)

# Reproduce Function
- Take 2 solutions and make them reproduce

# Mutation Function
- Flip random items in a population

# Selection Function
- Choosing the best solution (worst solutions will not survive)
